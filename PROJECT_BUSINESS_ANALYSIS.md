## 📦 Product Portfolio

### Two Main Brand Lines:

#### 1. **ARIVANO** - Luxury Extra Virgin Olive Oil
- **Positioning:** Ultra-premium, luxury tier
- **Production:** Limited edition (5,000 bottles per harvest)
- **Origin:** Single estate, Northern Tunisia
- **Process:** Cold-pressed within hours of harvest (<27°C)
- **Price Range:** €45-€120 per bottle
- **Target:** High-end restaurants, luxury retailers, collectors
- **Features:**
  - Hand-selected olives from century-old trees
  - Award-winning (Gold medal competitions)
  - Single estate origin
  - Bold, complex flavor profile
  - Tasting notes: Fresh grass, artichoke, green almond, pepper, herbaceous

#### 2. **OLIVORE** - Premium Olive Oil Line
- **Positioning:** Premium but accessible
- **Production:** Year-round availability
- **Origin:** Multi-source, select Tunisian varieties
- **Process:** Expertly blended, cold-pressed
- **Price Range:** €24-€35 per bottle
- **Target:** Everyday luxury consumers, restaurants, retailers
- **Features:**
  - Versatile for cooking and finishing
  - Mild, balanced flavor profile
  - Certified quality standards
  - Tasting notes: Mild fruit, butter, ripe olive, smooth, balanced

## 🏭 Production Process

### 4-Step Journey: From Grove to Bottle

1. **Harvest**
   - Hand-picked at perfect ripeness
   - Centuries-old groves in Tunisia's finest regions
   - Expert harvesters select optimal timing

2. **Selection**
   - Meticulous quality control
   - Only premium fruit proceeds
   - Batch-by-batch evaluation

3. **Cold Press**
   - Within hours of harvest
   - Temperature below 27°C
   - Preserves flavors and nutritional properties

4. **Refinement**
   - Master blenders craft perfect balance
   - Meets exacting CELIUM standards
   - Quality testing before bottling

## ✅ Quality Standards & Certifications

### Quality Pillars:
1. **Careful Selection:** Hand-picked at optimal ripeness
2. **Uncompromising Purity:** 100% natural, zero additives
3. **Excellence Certified:** Rigorous testing meets international standards
4. **Artisanal Process:** Traditional methods with modern precision

### Testing Process:
- **Chemical Analysis:** Free acidity, peroxide value, UV absorbance, fatty acid composition
- **Sensory Evaluation:** Trained panelists assess aroma, taste, mouthfeel
- **Freshness Testing:** Oxidation monitoring, optimal storage conditions

### Certifications:
- ISO 22000 Food Safety
- HACCP Certified
- IOC Standards Compliant (International Olive Council)
- EU Quality Mark
- Organic Certification Available
- Halal Certified

### Quality Guarantee:
- 100% Authentic (Pure Tunisian, never blended)
- Fresh Guarantee (Optimal shelf life)
- Traceability (Grove to bottle transparency)
